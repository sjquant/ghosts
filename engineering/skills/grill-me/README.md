# Grill Me

An interview that stress-tests a plan, decision, or idea through question rounds
ordered by their dependencies, ending with a short, grouped decision summary.

## Credits

Shout-out to [Matt Pocock](https://github.com/mattpocock) for the original
[grilling skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md).
This adaptation keeps its interview flow and adds guidance for a concise,
readable result summary.
