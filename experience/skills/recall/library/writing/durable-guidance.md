# Write guidance around the reader’s current decision

When writing a policy, operating guide or reusable knowledge document, explain the behavior and conditions readers need now. Define unfamiliar domain terms instead of assuming readers share the context.

### Before

```text
Legacy records were backfilled during the migration.
```

### After

```text
The recorded amount can differ from the amount actually billed. Check the
billing report when reconciling a payment.
```

The reusable caveat and next action survive; the completed migration history does not help the reader decide.

Write conditions and cause-and-effect relationships in complete sentences when compressed noun labels hide them.
