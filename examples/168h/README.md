# 168h weekly planner

Open `index.html` in a browser. The sample week accounts for 56 hours of sleep, 40 of work, 24 of life, 18 of focus, and 30 available hours. Add a task to the backlog or schedule it in a free slot; edits are saved locally for each week. On mobile, choose a day from the week overview, then tap an open slot or a backlog task to plan it.

This is an evaluation build for `ghosts:ui-design`. Codex imagegen explored the desktop direction and mobile flow. `DESIGN.md` links the selected desktop concept and verified mobile screens; the concept's numbers are illustrative. The working screen computes totals and available-slot suggestions from its schedule and rejects overlapping or over-midnight blocks.
