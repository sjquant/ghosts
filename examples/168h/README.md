# 168h weekly planner

Open `index.html` in a browser. The sample week accounts for 56 hours of sleep, 40 of work, 24 of life, 18 of focus, and 30 available hours. Add a task to the backlog or schedule it in a free slot; edits are saved locally for each week.

This is an evaluation build for `ghosts:ui-design`. The generated preview in `DESIGN.md` guided the visual direction. The working screen computes totals from its schedule and rejects overlapping or over-midnight blocks.
